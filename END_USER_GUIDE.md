# Enterprise End-User Guide: Multimodal Offline RAG

## 1. Getting Started
Welcome to the Enterprise Multimodal Offline RAG Platform. This workspace lets you ask questions and gain deep insights across corporate documents, presentations, scanned reports, and diagrams with guaranteed citation accuracy and privacy.

---

## 2. Asking Questions & AI Chat
1. **Starting a Conversation**: Click **+ New Chat** in the left navigation sidebar.
2. **Multilingual Querying**: Ask questions in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, French, German, or Arabic. The system automatically preserves cross-lingual retrieval fidelity.
3. **Model Selection**: The bottom-left status card displays the active Answer Model:
   - **Local Qwen (`qwen3:4b`)**: 100% on-premises offline processing with zero cloud data transmission.
   - **Cloud Providers (Claude, OpenAI, Groq, Gemini)**: High-speed cloud reasoning with verified API credentials.

---

## 3. Multimodal Ingestion & Audio Queries
- **Attaching Files**: Click the **Attachment (+) icon** in the composer.
  - **Documents**: PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, CSV, Excel.
  - **Images & Diagrams**: PNG, JPG, WEBP (analyzed via CLIP embeddings and BLIP image captioning).
  - **Audio Dictation**: Click the **Microphone icon** to record a voice query:
    - Transcribed locally using OpenAI Whisper.
    - Dictated text is inserted into the prompt box for review and editing before you press Send.

---

## 4. Evidence Viewer & Citation Verification
Every factual claim generated by the system includes an inline numbered citation badge (e.g. `[1]`, `[2]`).

1. **Clicking a Citation**: Clicking any citation chip opens the **Split-Screen Evidence Viewer**.
2. **Page-Level Navigation**:
   - For **PDF documents**, the viewer opens directly to the exact page where the passage appears.
   - For **text documents (DOCX, TXT)**, verified text-level evidence is displayed.
3. **Exact Supporting Passage Highlight**:
   - The supporting sentence or paragraph is highlighted in amber/yellow inside the document viewer.
   - If the exact sentence is slightly formatted, fuzzy phrase matching ensures the supporting passage is clearly visible.
4. **Annotations Drawer**:
   - On desktop, view all cited passages in the right-hand panel.
   - On mobile/tablet, tap **Annotations** or use the slide-over bottom sheet to inspect sources without squeezing the document.

---

## 5. Collections & Knowledge Workspaces
- Organize related documents into custom folders called **Collections** (e.g., "Legal Compliance", "Quarterly Financials").
- Select a collection in the chat scope dropdown to restrict answers exclusively to documents within that collection.
- Shared collections allow teams to collaborate on common research corpora.
